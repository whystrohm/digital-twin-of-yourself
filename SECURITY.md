# Security

## Report a problem

Write to hello@whystrohm.com with "Digital Twin security" in the subject. Please do not
open a public issue. You will get a reply within three working days.

## What this repo does with your data

- The scripts run on your machine. They make no network requests.
- `twin_scan.py` strips emails, phone numbers and money amounts before it saves anything.
- The repo ships with no keys and asks for none.

## What to check before you share a twin

A twin is built from your writing. Read [`SAFETY_CHECKLIST.md`](SAFETY_CHECKLIST.md)
before you publish one or paste it into a tool you do not control.
