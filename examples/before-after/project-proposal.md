# Opening Message: Warm Referral

Fictional. The Twin here is the sample Founder/CEO profile, an invented founder of a made-up company. See [founder-ceo.md](../sample-profiles/founder-ceo.md).

## Scenario
A friend introduces the founder to the owner of a six-person bike shop that wants to sell repair kits.

## The Prompt
"A friend just introduced you to a bike shop owner who wants to sell repair kits. Write your opening message."

## Without a Twin (Generic AI Output)

Hi [Name],

Great to connect! [Friend] spoke really highly of you and your shop. I'd love to learn more about what you're building.

A little about us: we're a passionate team on a mission to help people get more out of their gear. We've worked with a range of retailers and we're always excited to find new partners.

I think there could be a really exciting opportunity here. Would you be open to a quick call? I'm flexible on timing, whatever works best for you.

Looking forward to connecting!

## With a Twin Active (Founder/CEO Profile)

[Name],

[Friend] said your customers keep asking you to patch tubes they could patch themselves.

Two options. We send you a starter box of tube and tyre kits, you put them by the till, and we look at what sold after a month. Or we come by on a Saturday and run a twenty-minute repair demo for your customers first.

Which one fits your shop?

## What Changed
- **Opening:** The Twin starts with the shop's problem. The generic version starts with a greeting and a paragraph about the company.
- **Decision logic:** "Can we ship it in a week?" again: a starter box, not a partnership plan.
- **Signature moves:** "Two options." Repair language (patch, kits). Small numbers in time, not money. One question at the end.
- **Anti-patterns avoided:** No "excited", no "passionate team on a mission", no "whatever works best for you".
