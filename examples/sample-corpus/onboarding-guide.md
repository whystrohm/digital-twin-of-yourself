# Packing floor: first day

Read this before your first shift. It takes five minutes.

## The rules

- One owner per task. Your name on the board means it is yours.
- Write the problem down before you fix it. The log is the memory.
- Ask early. A question at 9am is cheap. A mistake at 4pm is not.
- Leave the bench clean. The next person starts from your bench.

## Your first morning

You shadow a packer until lunch. Watch how they read the pick list. Watch how they check the count twice.

After lunch you pack alone. Your trainer stays on the floor. Wave if you are stuck.

## When something breaks

Stop. Write it in the log. Tell the shift lead. Then fix it, or wait for help.

That is the job.
