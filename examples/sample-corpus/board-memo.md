# Memo to the board: warehouse expansion

I think it is probably worth considering whether the current warehouse footprint will remain adequate over the next eighteen months, given that order volume has perhaps grown faster than we initially anticipated and that the packing floor is, in my opinion, beginning to show some early signs of strain during the busier weeks of the quarter.

There are several options that the board might want to weigh — leasing the adjacent unit, moving to a second shift, or possibly outsourcing a portion of the slower-moving stock to a third-party facility — and each of these options carries a somewhat different mix of cost, risk and disruption that we would need to examine carefully before committing.

It seems like the second shift could be the least disruptive option in the near term, although it would maybe require us to hire and train additional packers, and the training load on the existing team is already fairly significant at this point in the year.

I would welcome the board's thoughts on which of these directions feels most aligned with the longer-term plan, and I am happy to prepare a more detailed comparison of the three options for the next meeting if that would be useful.
